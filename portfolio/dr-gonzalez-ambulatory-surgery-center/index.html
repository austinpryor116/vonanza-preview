NEW ASC MEDICAL OFFICE BUILDING

DR. GONZALEZ AMBULATORY SURGERY CENTER

// SITE LOCATION1480 Redd Rd, El Paso, TX
// FOOTPRINT METRIC6,200 SQFT
// TIMELINE / COMPLETION5 Months (Completed May 2021)
// STERILIZATION STANDARD1 OR, 7 Exams, Sterile Storage
// INFRASTRUCTURE GAS PIPINGMed-Gas, Nurse Call, OR Lights
// CLINICAL CONTINUITY & UPTIMENew Construction
// BUILD CLASSIFICATIONNew ASC Medical Office Building
// PROJECT SUMMARY

Construction of a 6,200 sq ft state-of-the-art medical facility for a plastic surgeon designed to meet ASC (Ambulatory Surgery Center) standards. Clinical areas feature 7 exam rooms, an operating room (OR), pre-op and post-op areas with a 3-bed capacity each, a pharmacy/nurse's station, and a soiled decontamination workroom. Administrative spaces include 5 offices, an electrical/IT room, a photo room, and a records room. Special systems installed include medical gas distribution compliant with HHSC inspection and certification, nurse call systems, and specialized surgical lighting in the OR, supported by clean/sterile storage facilities.
