MISSION & CAPABILITIES

LEADERSHIP & EXPERTISE

At Vonanza Construction, we deliver more than commercial buildings—we help clients transform ideas into successful, high-performing investments.

From site selection and preconstruction planning to construction management and project completion, we provide the leadership, technical expertise, and strategic guidance needed to deliver complex commercial and healthcare projects with confidence.

Our team specializes in healthcare, dental, professional, retail, and commercial facilities, combining decades of construction experience with meticulous planning, regulatory compliance, and quality craftsmanship. Whether developing a new medical facility, renovating an occupied healthcare environment, or constructing a commercial space from the ground up, we manage every phase with precision, transparency, and accountability.

For healthcare projects, we coordinate specialized systems including medical gas, mechanical, electrical, and plumbing infrastructure while ensuring compliance with applicable healthcare codes and industry standards. Our goal is not simply to build facilities—it is to create environments that support operational efficiency, patient safety, and long-term value.

SEC.01 // EXECUTIVE BOARD
OFFICE OF THE CEO

CYNTHIA ZARAGOZA

PRESIDENT & CEO

Construction has always been more than a profession for Cynthia Zaragoza—it is a family legacy. Raised in Chihuahua, Mexico, Cynthia developed her passion for building at an early age alongside her father, a respected builder who shaped some of the region's largest residential developments. Those foundational experiences instilled the core values of integrity, discipline, and the belief that every successful project begins with meticulous planning. After relocating to El Paso, Texas, in 2001, Cynthia built a 35-year career through an unwavering commitment to excellence, successfully leading commercial projects across healthcare, retail, industrial, and tenant improvement sectors. Today, as Founder and CEO of Vonanza Construction, Cynthia’s role extends far beyond traditional execution. She serves as a strategic real estate development partner—guiding investors and medical practitioners from site evaluation and land acquisition through preconstruction budgeting and final build. By leveraging data-driven decision-making and connecting clients with qualified capital resources, Cynthia actively reduces risk, maximizes ROI, and creates long-term value for every facility she builds.
