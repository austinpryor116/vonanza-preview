FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

CLINICAL RIGOR FAQ

What is your approach to ground-up medical and dental clinic construction?

+
We deliver comprehensive turn-key design-build services. Our team manages the entire process from site selection, land feasibility, and municipal zoning permits to architectural planning, structural shell engineering, and complete medical gas integrations. This integrated workflow reduces design cycles and ensures your facility is fully validated and ready for occupancy on schedule.

How do you maintain sterile air quality and infection control during clinical fit-outs?

+
We deploy rigorous ICRA (Infection Control Risk Assessment) protocols during all interior construction phases of a new build. We establish airtight physical containment barriers, deploy negative-pressure air machines with certified HEPA scrubbers, and conduct continuous real-time airborne particulate monitoring to guarantee that the building's HVAC and clinical areas remain pristine before initial occupancy.

How do you ensure patient and staff safety during active clinical construction?

+
Patient and staff safety is our absolute priority. We construct rigid, noise-attenuating barrier walls to fully isolate work areas, establish dedicated debris egress paths that bypass patient-facing corridors, and enforce strict negative-pressure HEPA filtration. Any high-vibration work is scheduled off-peak, and all operations comply with healthcare safety guidelines, preventing cross-contamination and maintaining a secure, quiet healing environment.
